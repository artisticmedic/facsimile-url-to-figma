↑Pin me — click here to capture
1

Allow it on local files

To capture local HTML files (file://), open chrome://extensions → Facsimile → Details, and turn on Allow access to file URLs. This lets you capture local builds, not just live sites.

2

Click the loader to copy

The first time you capture on a site, Chrome asks for clipboard permission. When the page-side bar shows a spinner, click it once to grant access and copy to your clipboard — after that it's automatic.
